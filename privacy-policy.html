LandHalo Privacy Policy

Effective October 23, 2026

LandHalo is a land presentation camera for iPhone and iPad, published by Baran Baran ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app. The short version: LandHalo has no app account, advertising or cross-app tracking. Your properties and media stay on your device. Anonymous purchase information is processed by Apple and RevenueCat as described below.

Information stored on your device

Properties, parcel boundaries, calibration data, photos, recordings, exports, branding details (company, agent name, phone, website, logo) and settings are stored only in LandHalo's private storage on your device. Media files are encrypted at rest with iOS Data Protection. We never receive this information. It is included in your device backups according to your iOS settings, and it is deleted when you delete a property or the app.

Camera, location and photos

  • Camera is used while you use the AR view to show the boundary and record videos.
  • Location is used while you use maps or AR, to align the parcel with the real world and show your position. It is not stored with your data and not sent to us.
  • Photos access is add-only: LandHalo writes a photo or video to your library only when you tap Save. You choose videos to import with the system picker; LandHalo cannot browse your library.

You can change these permissions at any time in iOS Settings.

Apple services

Map imagery, 3D maps, address suggestions for new parcels and Apple location anchors are provided by Apple. To render them, the map area or coordinates you view are sent to Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy.

Subscriptions and Lifetime Pro purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat, Inc. to validate purchases and restore access to LandHalo Pro, including monthly and annual subscriptions and the one-time, non-consumable Lifetime Pro purchase. RevenueCat receives an anonymous, randomly generated app user ID and your purchase history (products, dates and transaction identifiers) from the App Store. We do not send RevenueCat your name, email, property content, location or payment card details. Purchase history is used to enable features, prevent fraud, process entitlement changes such as refunds, and provide purchase reporting and analytics in RevenueCat’s dashboard. It is not linked by us to your real-world identity or used for advertising tracking. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

Sharing

LandHalo shares files only when you do so yourself, for example with the share sheet or AirDrop. We do not sell or share personal information, and we do not track you across apps or websites.

Children

LandHalo is a professional tool and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your rights

Because your content is stored only on your device, you control it directly: edit or delete properties and media in the app, or delete the app to remove everything. For questions about purchase data held by RevenueCat or Apple, contact us and we will help.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
