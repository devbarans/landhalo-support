The boundary isn't where it should be

GPS and the compass are only accurate to a few metres and degrees. Stand near a corner you can identify, choose that corner in calibration, aim the crosshair at it on the ground and tap Set Marker. Rotate until an edge lines up with a fence or road, then confirm. A second, distant corner improves orientation.

"Alignment may have drifted"

AR tracking accumulates error as you walk far. Tap Recalibrate and confirm a nearby corner again.

Importing a parcel

LandHalo imports Polygon and MultiPolygon shapes from KML (Google Earth, most cadastral portals) and GeoJSON files up to 20 MB, with up to 1,000 corners per parcel. Open the file from Files, Mail or AirDrop, or use New Property › Import.

Saving videos to Photos

If saving fails, allow LandHalo to add photos in iOS Settings › Apps › LandHalo › Photos.

Subscriptions

To restore LandHalo Pro on a new device, tap Restore Purchases on the subscription screen (also available in Settings › Subscription). To cancel, open iOS Settings › your name › Subscriptions. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
